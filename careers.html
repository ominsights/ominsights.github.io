Mission

Turn real lawyer conversations into a sharper product.

Talk to litigation lawyers, run demos, capture workflow pain, manage beta leads, and help translate customer feedback into product improvements for OmInsights.

What you will do

  • Speak with solo practitioners, small law firms, and legal teams in India.
  • Explain OmInsights in plain lawyer language and run short beta demos.
  • Capture how lawyers prepare before hearings: CNR checks, order review, file review, missing documents, client updates, and task follow-up.
  • Maintain CRM discipline across lead source, replies, demos, objections, next steps, and feedback notes.
  • Summarize customer feedback into clear product notes for the founding team.
  • Follow ethical outreach rules: public contacts only, opt-out respected, no scraped personal data.

What helps you succeed

  • Strong communication and careful follow-up.
  • Comfort speaking with lawyers and professional-service customers.
  • Telugu, Hindi, and English are useful for Andhra Pradesh and Telangana outreach.
  • Basic technical comfort with web app demos and issue reporting.
  • Startup ownership mindset: learn quickly, improve the script, and keep clean notes.
