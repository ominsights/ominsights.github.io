Invitation-Based Beta

Pricing and beta access

OmInsights is preparing a controlled, founder-led beta for Indian litigation users. Access is intentionally limited while workflows, cost controls, support, and trust surfaces are verified.

Current beta posture

Founding beta access is available by invitation. Paid pilot pricing is shared after fit confirmation and written pilot scope.

Stage What it means Payment status
Demo beta Controlled walkthroughs and safe demo data. No payment collected.
Real data beta Limited lawyer users, beta terms, deletion/export path, and founder support. No broad public pricing.
Paid pilot Written pilot scope, limits, support path, and invoice/payment process. Only after payment and GST readiness.

Paid pilot pricing depends on

  • Number of users.
  • Number of active matters.
  • Expected document volume and pages processed.
  • Expected AI usage and readiness packet generation.
  • Support and onboarding needs.

Usage limits

OmInsights will not offer unlimited AI or unlimited document processing during beta.

  • Active matters, uploads, pages, storage, AI actions, imports, and readiness packets may be limited.
  • Excessive usage may be throttled or paused to protect reliability and cost control.
  • Any paid pilot will receive written limits before payment begins.
