Why this is different

A readiness system, not a legal chatbot.

Chatbots give you answers you cannot verify. OmInsights gives you a workflow: court changes, documents, obligations, and lawyer decisions compounding into a system your chamber depends on every week.

Every answer shows its source

Court snapshots, linked documents, and AI citations remain inspectable. You never wonder where an answer came from.

AI output starts as unreviewed

Obligations, summaries, and inferences carry a trust state. Lawyer sign-off is a product feature, not an afterthought.

"Cannot determine" is an honest answer

The system says when it does not have enough information rather than inventing confidence.
