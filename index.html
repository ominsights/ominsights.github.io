First beta users

OmInsights is preparing for a controlled, founder-led India Legal beta.

  • Solo advocates handling active litigation matters.
  • Two-to-five lawyer chambers with repeated document review.
  • Teams that need source-linked readiness, not broad public AI access.
  • Users willing to test with a defined pilot period and candid feedback loop.

Trust posture

  • OmInsights is a productivity and matter-readiness tool.
  • OmInsights does not provide legal advice.
  • AI-generated outputs must be reviewed and verified by a qualified legal professional before use.
  • Beta matter data is not used to train models by default.
  • Deletion and export requests are handled through the privacy/support path.
  • Source links, gaps, and uncertainty are part of the product posture.

India-first beta operations

OmInsights is being prepared as an India-first trust product. The beta infrastructure target is AWS Mumbai, with payment, invoice, and GST readiness before paid beta.
