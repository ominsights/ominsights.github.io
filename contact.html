Business address

Business address will be published before paid beta and before live payment activation.

OmInsights will use the company registered office or an approved business mailing address once the entity and payment path are settled.

What to include in support requests

  • Your name and workspace email.
  • Organization or chamber name, if applicable.
  • Matter identifier, if relevant.
  • A short description of the issue.
  • Whether the request involves deletion, export, security, access, or billing.

Privacy and data rights

Use privacy@ominsights.in for access, correction, deletion, export, consent withdrawal, and retention questions.
